## 5. Data Subject Rights

### Right to Access
You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
- Self-service: Account Settings > "Download My Data"
- Email: privacy@manikgoldenhoney.com

### Right to Rectification
You can correct inaccurate personal data.
- Self-service: Update profile in Account Settings
- Email: support@manikgoldenhoney.com

### Right to Erasure
You can request deletion of your personal data.
- Self-service: Account Settings > "Delete My Account"
- Email: privacy@manikgoldenhoney.com
- 30-day cooling-off period applies

### Right to Restrict Processing
You can request we limit how we use your data.
- Email: privacy@manikgoldenhoney.com

### Right to Portability
You can receive your data in machine-readable format.
- Self-service: Account Settings > "Download My Data"
- Formats: JSON, CSV

### Right to Object
You can object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- Email: privacy@manikgoldenhoney.com
