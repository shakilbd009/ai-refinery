## 1. Order Cancellation Policy

**Based on ADR-003: Cancellation Request Workflow**

```
ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY

You may request cancellation of orders that have not yet shipped.

How to Cancel:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "My Orders"
3. Click "Request Cancellation" on the order
4. Provide a reason (optional but helpful)

Review Process:
- We review all cancellation requests within 24 hours
- We may contact you with alternatives (address change, product swap)
- Approved cancellations receive full refunds

Refund Timeline:
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days
- Refunds issued to original payment method

Non-Cancellable Orders:
- Orders marked as "Shipped" cannot be cancelled
- See our Return Policy for shipped items
```

- Contact us within 14 days of delivery
- We'll ship correct item immediately

- Unopened products: Full refund within 30 days
- Opened products: Contact us to discuss options

- Items returned after 30 days

How to Request:
- Email: support@manikgoldenhoney.com
- Subject: "Refund Request - Order #[number]"
- Include order number and reason

Processing Time:
- Refund decision within 3 business days
- Refund issued within 5-7 business days of approval

## 4. Review Guidelines

**Based on Review Moderation L3**

```
PRODUCT REVIEW GUIDELINES

We value honest customer feedback. To maintain helpful reviews for all
customers, please follow these guidelines:

Acceptable Reviews:
- Your honest opinion of the product
- Description of taste, quality, packaging
- How you use the honey
- Comparison to other products (without promoting competitors)

Not Acceptable (will be rejected):
- Reviews for products you haven't purchased
- Personal information (phone numbers, addresses, email addresses)
- External links or promotional content
- Profanity or offensive language
- False claims about health benefits
- Content unrelated to the product
- Spam or duplicate content

Review Moderation:
- All reviews are reviewed by our team before publishing
- Reviews typically approved within 24 hours
- Rejected reviews include a reason and opportunity to edit
- Multiple rejections may result in waiting periods between edits

Review Editing:
- You may edit your review after rejection
- Editing an approved review returns it to moderation
- This prevents "bait and switch" abuse

Review Removal:
- We may remove reviews that violate these guidelines
- We may remove reviews that contain false information
- Competitors attempting to damage our reputation will be reported
```
